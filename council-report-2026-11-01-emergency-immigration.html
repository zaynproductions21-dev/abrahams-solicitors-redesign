Consensus

  • Current page's detention-first framing is misaligned with real demand — visa-expired is 2× bigger by both volume AND won cases.
  • The 57% "other" + unspecified bucket is the biggest conversion ceiling on this page. Fix the taxonomy, not the hero.
  • Universal copy risk: "urgent" + "emergency" + outcome verbs breach SRA Code + Google government-docs policy. Reframe as bounded legal services and representation.
  • Sub-pages must be indexable (not noindex) with LegalService + FAQPage schema, named author, speakable targets, statute citations.
  • Sequential ship, not parallel. Three simultaneous changes with £300/day Smart Bidding running = money fire.

Key disagreement — resolved

Which sub-page first? 5 advisors said /immigration-detention/ (highest desperation + lowest competition + strongest organic E-E-A-T via Hardial Singh). 2 advisors said /visa-expired-urgent/ (fastest to Smart Bidding 30-conv threshold + 2/3 of won cases + lowest YMYL compliance landmine).

Chairman call: ship /visa-expired-urgent/ FIRST. The paid campaign is burning £300/day — the operational argument (fastest conversion signal + lowest compliance landmine) outweighs the strategic organic argument this week. Detention ships week 2 and owns the organic / SRA moat.

Blind spots identified

  1. No WhatsApp intake channel — mobile-90% visitors in crisis prefer text over call.
  2. Tablet is burning £107/mo at 0 conv — exclude via -100% bid immediately.
  3. No plan to import emergency_immigration_lead as a Google Ads conversion action — Smart Bidding will be blind for 2–4 weeks without this.
  4. No named duty solicitor — "24/7" without naming the rota implies regulated availability (SRA Principle 7).
  5. No panic-English translation of labels — "removal directions" / "refusal notice" are Home Office jargon.
  6. n=49 is small — statistical significance on a 3-arm A/B needs 2–4 weeks even with £300/day traffic.

Recommended changes

  1. Ship /visa-expired-urgent/ FIRST (not detention). Fastest conversion-signal to the Google Ads action; 2 of 3 won cases came from this bucket; lowest YMYL compliance landmine.
  2. Fix taxonomy before router. Day 1: PostHog emergency_immigration_lead event + rewrite situation dropdown + sticky huge phone number ABOVE the tiles. Day 3–5: ship /visa-expired-urgent/. Week 2: /immigration-detention/ once new event has ≥30 conv.
  3. Kill the "Something else" tile as proposed. Replace with "Not sure? → Call now" primary tap-to-call + free-text triage form that pre-fills "Tell us what's happened in your own words."
  4. Translate every tile label into panic-English. "Visa expired" → "My visa has run out". "Loved one in detention" → "Someone I know has been detained". "Removal or refusal notice" → "I've got a letter saying I have to leave".
  5. Copy guardrails: no "urgent" + outcome verbs. Replace with "same-day legal advice" + "representation". Name the duty solicitor with photo + SRA profile link. SRA badge. No "we obtain your visa" anywhere.
  6. Sub-pages indexable, with GEO/AI-citation structure. 150-word verbatim answer block under H1; FAQPage + LegalService schema with Imran Shah as named author; speakable CSS selectors; statute citations (section 3C IA 1971; Hardial Singh; Pre-Action Protocol).
  7. Operational hygiene before architecture. Exclude tablet (-100% bid) today. Import emergency_immigration_lead as a Google Ads conversion action BEFORE splitting ad groups. Add WhatsApp Business as a secondary CTA alongside phone.
